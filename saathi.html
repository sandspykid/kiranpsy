What Saathi never does

  • Name a diagnosis or a "probable" disorder
  • Tell you to start, stop or change a dose
  • Carry a crisis alone. Risk language opens the crisis card and alerts the clinic
  • Pretend to be a person. It says it is an AI at the start of every session
  • Keep anything from an anonymous session

How it is built

Safety first, assistant second, doctor last and always.

Every message passes through the same three stages. The order is the point.

1Safety gateA small, fast model reads every incoming message for self-harm, harm to others, abuse, psychosis, intoxication or a medical emergency. If it fires, the crisis card shows first and the on-call line is alerted. The main assistant is not consulted.
2Saathi assistantA larger model with tools: your appointment slots, your own prescription, the clinic FAQ, scale scoring and a report summariser. It answers in your language and keeps to its rules.
3Clinician reviewAnything that touches care, such as an intake summary, a note or a flagged report, lands in the doctor's review queue as a draft with provenance. Nothing enters your record until a doctor signs it.
