Step 1 of 6 · Who and what

Who is this visit for?

You can book for yourself, for a family member, or for your child.

What kind of visit?

In the clinic, or on video?

First visits work best in person. Follow-ups and second opinions work well on video, wherever you are in Saurashtra.

Video for a first visitIf this is a new problem and you live outside Bhavnagar, we will still see you on video. The doctor may ask for one in-person visit later for examination or an EEG.

Which doctor?

Both doctors see every condition. Pick by availability, or by focus.

Day and time

Clinic runs Monday to Saturday, 10:00-13:00 and 16:00-19:00. Your token is the order you are seen in; your arrive-by time is ten minutes before your slot.

Dashed slots are the first and last of a session: the quietest times in the waiting area. Struck-through slots are already booked.

Before a first visit

Write down the three or four things that trouble you most, with one example each. Bring old prescriptions in date order. If you can, complete the short form Saathi sends you so the doctor reads your story before you enter.

How to make the visit useful

Before an EEG

Wash your hair the night before and skip oil or gel. Take your usual medicines unless the doctor says otherwise. If a sleep-deprived EEG is ordered, you will be told exactly how late to stay up.

EEG and epilepsy clinic

Bringing a reluctant relative

Book the first slot of a session for a quiet waiting room. Tell them it is a "check-up for sleep and stress", which is true. The doctor will meet them where they are. You can brief the doctor through Saathi first.
