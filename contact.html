Getting here

Tell any auto driver "A Division Police Station".

By two-wheeler or car

Two-wheeler parking is in front of Samved Complex on Jail Road. Take the lift to the 3rd floor; the clinic is rooms 309 and 310. The stairs are there if the lift is busy.

By bus or auto

The landmark is A Division Police Station on Jail Road; we are directly opposite. From the ST bus stand or the railway station, ask for the police station and look for Samved Complex across the road.

From outside Bhavnagar

Mahuva, Palitana, Sihor, Talaja, Botad, Amreli: the first visit is best in person, and follow-ups can be on video so the bus ride is not the reason you drop out. Fees are shown at booking.

For referring physicians

Send a patient. Get a summary back.

Family physicians, paediatricians, neurologists, gynaecologists and surgeons across Saurashtra send us patients for depression after a heart event, delirium in the elderly, a first seizure, post-partum low mood, or a tobacco habit that is blocking surgery.

How the loop closes

You refer

This form, a WhatsApp message to +91 87582 99921, or a slip in the patient's hand. Mark it urgent and we call you back the same session.

We confirm to you and to the patient

A token, a date and an arrive-by time go to the patient on WhatsApp, and a confirmation goes to you.

A summary is returned to you

After the first visit, with the patient's consent: working diagnosis, plan, medicines started, what to watch for, and when to send them back. Nothing goes without that consent, under the Mental Healthcare Act 2017.
