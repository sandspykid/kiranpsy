Measurement-based care

We measure. We do not guess.

Every follow-up starts with a two-minute check-in on your phone: PHQ-9 for mood, GAD-7 for anxiety, ISI for sleep, Y-BOCS for OCD, Fagerström and AUDIT for addiction. Your doctor sees the trend, not just today's answer, and adjusts treatment on evidence.

Myths & facts

Tap a card. The myth flips.

All myths and facts
Myth

Once you start psychiatric medicines, you can never stop them.

Tap to flip
Fact

Most courses have an end. How long depends on your history and how fully you recover. Stopping is planned with your doctor, step by step, never suddenly.

Tap to flip back
Myth

Mental illness is a weakness, not a medical condition.

Tap to flip
Fact

It is a medical condition like diabetes or heart disease, with biological, psychological and social causes. Most people improve substantially with the right treatment.

Tap to flip back
Myth

A counsellor and a psychiatrist are the same thing.

Tap to flip
Fact

A psychiatrist is a medical doctor with 12 or more years of training who can diagnose, prescribe and treat the physical causes too. Counselling often works alongside, not instead.

Tap to flip back
Myth

Fits and seizures are a mental illness or a spirit.

Tap to flip
Fact

Epilepsy is a neurological condition from abnormal electrical activity in the brain. An EEG helps diagnose it and most seizures are controlled with medicine.
